WHAT RUNNING THINGS FOUND THAT READING DID NOT
================================================================

1. PyYAML implements YAML 1.1, where bare 'on' is the boolean True. The key
   join.on therefore parsed as True and the join vanished, silently, in a rule
   that otherwise looked correct. The loader now resolves only true/false as
   booleans. Schema section 1.

2. Under schema 1.0.0 the CD-012 check could not run at all, because
   docs/details-schema.md did not exist on the branch it was written against.
   It exists at 0994251 and the check is live: R-TEST-0003's reference to
   details.not_declared_anywhere is rejected, while details.executable,
   details.value_name and details.sha1 all resolve.

3. Two defect-log entries changed the schema rather than merely informing it.
   D-027 (persistence predates its own download by two days) produced sections
   4.7 and 5.4 and two new load-time checks. D-028 (the scenario binary has no
   Amcache entry) produced section 6.2 and the optional/amcache check.

4. Writing the six rules opened two items for Person B, both in section 11:
   the 4104 script-text gap above, and regipy - imported by registry.py and
   amcache.py, declared in pyproject.toml by neither. It is imported lazily
   inside a function, so nothing fails at import time; the failure arrives when
   a parser runs, which is the worse of the two failure modes.

5. R-ATOMIC-0001 cannot detect the Scenario 1 binary, and that is a property of
   the evidence. The EID 4688 for invoice_scan.exe was destroyed by the log
   clear at 23:05:06. Prefetch is the only surviving witness and carries no
   path - its Event.path holds a bare executable name, declared as such in
   details.path_source. Week 4 must not count that silence as a miss.
