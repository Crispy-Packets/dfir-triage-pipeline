7. Other things writing the engine found
================================================================

1. A bug in the schema. Section 4.2 said the image token is the LONGEST
   leading prefix ending in an executable extension. It is the shortest --
   for 'run.exe -config C:\other\thing.exe' the longest runs to an argument.
   Corrected in 1.3.0, pinned by test_shortest_leading_prefix_not_longest.

2. A sentence was becoming an executable name. 'run this thing please'
   resolved to an entity.exe_name of itself, so two unrelated events
   carrying the same prose would have joined on it. Step 5 of the extraction
   now rejects a candidate with whitespace that does not end in an image
   extension.

3. PT45M is 28 milliseconds from spanning the whole scenario. Arrival to the
   EID 1102 is 44m 59.971593s. Nothing is wrong -- a log clear is not a
   process_exec, so the select prevents it -- but the margin is thinner than
   it looks and R-CORR-0001's justification now says so.

4. Chain 4 cannot join on an executable. A log-clearing record carries no
   path, so R-CORR-0004 joins on entity.user. Side effect: the LocalSystem
   and NT service accounts behind the Guest Additions services (D-026) are
   not principals under section 4.4, so they drop out before evaluation.

5. The schema has no aggregation operator, and chain 2 needs one. R-CORR-0002
   is written as one failure followed by a success rather than a burst, and
   says so. Recorded in schema section 11.

6. D-032's sixth ImagePath form is handled, but it was handled by accident:
   the extraction takes a basename and never roots anything. Written into
   section 4.2 and pinned in test_entity.py so it is now true by design.
